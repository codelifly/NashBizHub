NashBizHub Business Platform Proposal
Detailed client scope for buying selling renting and contract opportunities
Prepared for NashBizHub
23 October 2026
Version 1 for scope review
NashBizHub will bring business owners, buyers, tenants, operators and brokers into one platform. Customers will register once, choose what they want to do, and receive a guided experience from discovery through inquiry, negotiation and handover.
This proposal describes the complete proposed product in plain language. It includes public website pages, customer accounts, business listings, rental and contract workflows, staff operations, administration, reporting and the supporting React and Laravel PHP technology.
All names, listings, figures, dates and records in the examples are fictional. They demonstrate how the proposed system would work and are not real opportunities or market valuations. No development charges, subscription prices or implementation quotation are included.
The document is a proposed scope, not a statement that these features already exist. It separates the full core system from optional expansion modules and service integrations that depend on provider availability.

How to interpret the scope
Core means proposed as part of the complete NashBizHub platform. Core work can still be delivered in successive releases so that each workflow can be demonstrated and tested properly. Optional means an expansion that can be enabled without changing the main business model. Provider dependent means an external account, service approval or integration is required.
The proposed starting assumption is an English language, mobile responsive marketplace with configurable countries, currencies and time zones. Illustrative examples use Tennessee and US dollars, but the launch country is not fixed by this proposal. Country specific forms and approved agreement templates must be configured for each enabled market.
NashBizHub is assumed to facilitate introductions and manage transaction records. It does not automatically become the owner, landlord, employer, licensed intermediary or holder of customer transaction funds. If the business intends to perform any of those roles, the relevant operating process must be defined separately before activation.
The platform will have one shared customer identity with multiple roles. A person may buy one business, sell another and apply to operate a third without creating duplicate accounts.
1 Business purpose and proposed outcome
NashBizHub should work as a business opportunity marketplace and a transaction coordination system. The public website attracts customers and helps them compare opportunities. The private workspace helps the parties progress from initial interest to an agreed outcome. Staff receive a clear view of outstanding actions rather than relying on scattered emails and spreadsheets.
What customers should be able to achieve
Buyers find businesses that match their location, industry, experience and investment requirements, then contact the right owner or broker.
Owners advertise a business for sale, offer it to an operator on rent, or invite proposals for a management contract.
Landlords advertise business premises without implying that an operating business is included.
Operators describe their experience, submit applications and manage accepted rental or management agreements.
Contract providers publish a defined scope of work and compare eligible contractor proposals.
Brokers represent authorized clients and coordinate multiple listings and conversations.
What NashBizHub should gain
The system should create a consistent record of every listing, customer, inquiry, appointment, offer and agreement. Management should be able to identify unanswered leads, incomplete listings, expiring agreements and the sources that generate qualified interest.
Success should be measured through useful outcomes: published listings with complete information, timely responses, qualified applications, accepted offers, completed handovers and repeat customers. A high number of website visits alone is not evidence of successful transactions.
Example
Harbor Foods owns two fictional cafÃ©s. It offers one for sale and invites an operator to manage the other. The same company account holds both opportunities, but each has different fields, applicants and agreement requirements. A buyer cannot accidentally submit a purchase offer through the operator application form.
Completion standard
A customer should always know which opportunity type they are viewing, what action is available, what information they must provide and what happens next. Staff should be able to trace who changed a record and why.

Separate ownership from use
For a sale, the listing identifies what is included and whether it concerns assets or an ownership interest. For a business rental, it states which equipment, brand rights, stock, premises rights and responsibilities are included. For premises, it explains space and permitted uses. A management contract identifies who retains ownership and who is responsible for operations.
Linked opportunities
An owner can advertise a business for sale and as a rental alternative. These should be linked offers under one business record, not unrelated duplicate listings. The system warns the owner when one arrangement progresses far enough to conflict with another. Closing one offer can pause the alternatives after confirmation.
Contract interpretation
This proposal covers both meanings of contract basis: running another person's business under a management arrangement and bidding for a service contract. They share accounts and messaging but have separate applications, comparison criteria and completion records.
Example
A laundromat offered with machines and operating rights is a business rental. An empty shop suitable for a laundromat is a premises lease. Search filters, labels and forms must preserve that difference throughout the system.

Customer journey
The customer enters basic details, verifies their email and arrives at a dashboard tailored to their selected interest. They can add other interests later. If registration began from a listing, the system returns them to that listing and preserves their unfinished inquiry.
Login includes password recovery, session management and clear error messages. Recovery links expire and can be used once. An existing email should lead to sign in or recovery instead of creating another customer. Unverified accounts may save drafts but cannot publish or send unrestricted messages. Repeated verification attempts are limited. Social sign in is an optional convenience and must not silently merge accounts without confirming ownership.
Example

Acceptance example
After a password reset, expired links fail safely, the new password works and the user can revoke other active sessions. A registration error does not erase fields already completed.

Submitted evidence enters a queue. A reviewer approves, requests corrections or declines with a reason. Expired evidence triggers re-review and removes the corresponding current badge. 7 Listing creation and publication
Listing creation should be a guided sequence with automatic draft saving, a progress indicator and a final preview. Customers see fields relevant to their chosen opportunity type and industry.
Guided steps
Select the opportunity type and the business or property it concerns.
Confirm ownership or authority to advertise.
Enter a public title, category, location and summary.
Complete the relevant financial, rental or contract fields.
Upload public images and separately upload private documents.
Set confidentiality, contact preferences and application requirements.
Review the public preview, declare accuracy and submit for moderation.
The platform gives each listing a reference such as NBH-S-1001. Missing required fields are explained beside the field. The preview shows exactly what a visitor sees, including hidden address or undisclosed financial labels.
Publication states
Draft becomes Submitted, then Under Review. A moderator can request Changes, Reject with a reason or Approve for publication. Approved listings may be Active, Paused, Under Negotiation, Completed, Expired, Withdrawn or Suspended. Completion labels depend on type: Sold, Rented, Leased or Contract Awarded.
Editing a live listing
Minor approved changes can publish immediately under policy. Material changes to ownership, transaction type, financial figures or confidential identity return to review. The last approved version remains visible until replacement approval unless safety or accuracy requires immediate withdrawal. Each revision records author, time and reason.
Example
An owner uploads a photograph showing the cafÃ©'s name while requesting confidentiality. The reviewer returns the listing with a specific correction request. The owner replaces the photo and resubmits without re-entering the entire form.
Completion standard
No listing becomes public because a customer merely purchased promotion. Approval, publication and payment states are independent. Rejected or suspended content cannot reappear through an automated renewal job.

Buyer workflow
The buyer searches and saves opportunities, sends an inquiry and supplies the requested qualification. After any confidentiality agreement and owner approval, they review documents, arrange a visit and submit an offer. Negotiations proceed through recorded versions. The deal then moves into checks, agreement preparation and completion tasks.
Seller workflow
The seller reviews interest, assigns an owner to each lead, requests relevant information and decides which buyers receive documents. They can decline an inquiry with a professional response. Accepted preliminary terms do not automatically mark the business sold.

11 Commercial premises leasing
Commercial premises require a separate form and search category because the customer may receive only space, with no customers, staff, brand or trading business.
Property information
Record property type, location, floor area with units, available area, permitted or proposed use, existing fit-out, access, parking, accessibility information, utilities, availability date and viewing contact. The landlord can hide the exact address until a viewing is approved.
Commercial information
State the rent basis and period, deposit terms, proposed lease length, renewal options, outgoings, fit-out responsibilities, maintenance obligations and whether amounts include relevant taxes. These fields describe the offer and must not be presented as a standardized legal lease.
Lease application
Applicants provide business activity, proposed use, intended occupancy date, organization details, supporting references and any requested guarantor information. Sensitive evidence is visible only to authorized reviewers. The landlord can request additional information and record proposed changes.
Linked business sale or rental
A business sale may depend on obtaining a new premises lease or consent to an existing arrangement. The two records should be linked with a dependency task. Staff must be able to see that the business transaction is awaiting premises confirmation without treating that condition as already fulfilled.
Fictional example
NBH-L-2101 is a 1,800 square foot retail unit in Nashville. A cafÃ© operator seeks a five-year term and requests confirmation that the intended food preparation use is acceptable. The premises listing does not claim to include a trading cafÃ© or customer base.
Handover
The workspace records condition evidence, keys, access devices, meter readings, agreed fit-out tasks and responsible contacts. Renewal and expiry reminders use the agreement's dates rather than the advertisement's publication period.
Completion standard
The search interface can compare premises by area and rent basis without mixing those figures with business asking prices or operating business rentals.

Proposal workflow
The owner publishes the opportunity and closing date. Applicants ask questions through a controlled channel. Clarifications that affect all bidders are distributed consistently. A bidder saves a draft and submits before the deadline. The system records the exact submitted version and time zone.
The owner compares eligible submissions, records assessment notes, shortlists applicants and issues an award or decline. A correction after the deadline follows an explicit controlled process, not a silent overwrite. Sealed bids, if selected, remain inaccessible until the published opening point.
Fictional management example
NBH-M-3001 seeks an operator for a 32-room motel for 36 months. Applicants submit staffing, maintenance and guest-service plans. Performance measures include reporting timeliness and agreed service standards; the system does not guarantee occupancy.
Fictional service example
NBH-T-4001 invites cleaning proposals for five stores over 12 months. The tender includes service frequency, site schedules, evidence requirements and an evaluation checklist. Applicants cannot see competitors' quotations.
Completion standard
An award creates an agreement workspace. It does not automatically authorize payment, prove work completion or create an employment relationship.
13 Search comparison and opportunity matching
Search should make meaningful differences easy to understand. The selected opportunity type determines which filters and comparison fields are shown.
Search controls
Customers can filter by keyword, industry, category, country, state, city, radius, opportunity type, disclosed amount range, date added and selected operational characteristics. Sales can add revenue and earnings filters. Rentals can add term and commencement date. Contracts can add submission deadline, duration and capability requirements.
List and map views should agree about which results are included. Confidential listings use an approximate area and must never expose precise coordinates through the page data. Sorting can include newest, relevant match and applicable amount. Sponsored results are labeled and not misrepresented as the best commercial match.
Comparison and saved activity
Customers can compare a manageable number of opportunities side by side. Unknown figures remain unknown. Different currencies require explicit conversion context or separate comparison; the platform should not silently treat USD and AUD amounts as equivalent. Monthly rents and annual revenue must retain their labels.
Saved searches retain criteria, notification frequency and active status. A customer can pause or delete alerts. New matches, material changes and reopened listings should use distinct notification rules to avoid repeated duplicates.
Requirements board
A registered buyer or operator can submit a requirement such as restaurant businesses in a selected region, experience level and desired timeframe. Public requirements use approved summaries; personal contact details and financial evidence remain private. Owners respond with linked opportunities, and customers can reject irrelevant approaches.
Example
Maya saves a search for cafÃ©s within 30 miles of Nashville and a stated acquisition range. She receives a daily digest with three new matches, not three separate emails for edits to the same listing.
Completion standard
Matching explains the reasons for a result, such as location and industry. It must not describe an algorithmic recommendation as a financial endorsement or guaranteed fit.
14 Listing detail pages and customer actions
Each detail page should answer three questions: what is offered, what is known about it and how the customer can progress. The page layout changes by opportunity type while keeping familiar navigation.

A document request should explain the next step, such as completing qualification or accepting a confidentiality agreement.
Example

Status behavior
Sold or awarded pages should stop accepting new offers. Depending on the owner's publication preference, they can show a limited completion notice or be removed from browsing. Existing participants retain authorized access to their transaction history.

16 Confidentiality agreements and document rooms
The document room is a private workspace for approved participants. It should support staged disclosure rather than giving every registered customer immediate access to all business records.
Access sequence
The customer requests access, completes any qualification, receives the applicable confidentiality agreement, signs through the selected process and waits for owner approval where required. Access is granted to specified folders or documents with an expiry. A signed confidentiality agreement alone must not bypass the owner's approval rules.
Document organization
Typical folders include financial reports, asset schedules, premises documents, operating procedures, workforce summaries, licenses or permissions, supplier information and agreement drafts. Every file has an owner, version, upload date, description, access level and retention rule. Superseded files remain traceable but are clearly labeled.
Controls
Owners can grant, restrict or revoke access, set download permissions where supported, watermark generated copies and review access activity. Links expire and are tied to the authorized account. The system should explain that screenshots and already downloaded copies cannot be recalled by revoking future access.
Identity evidence, unrelated employee details and customer databases should not be shared by default. Redacted versions can be prepared for early review, with fuller disclosure restricted to a later approved stage.
Fictional example
Maya signs confidentiality agreement NDA-501 for Riverside CafÃ©. The owner approves read access to annual financial summaries and an asset list for 14 days. Payroll records remain unavailable. When the owner uploads a corrected report, the room shows version 2 and notifies authorized reviewers.
Completion standard
A person with another buyer's file link receives no access. Removing a team member immediately blocks their future document access. Audit records show grants, revocations, views and downloads that the system can reliably observe.
Agreement wording and signing requirements must be approved for the enabled jurisdiction. The software manages the process; it does not determine legal sufficiency automatically.
17 Appointments visits and discussions
Appointments should connect interested customers with owners, brokers and staff while keeping availability and confidentiality under control.
Booking information
Collect listing reference, appointment type, proposed time, time zone, duration, participants, meeting method, accessibility needs and notes. Supported types include telephone discussion, video meeting, site visit, document review and handover inspection.
The organizer can publish available slots or request manual confirmation. The system checks conflicting confirmed appointments, adds travel or preparation buffers where configured and distinguishes Requested from Confirmed. A calendar invitation should contain only information authorized for the invited participants.
Customer journey
The customer requests a slot. The organizer confirms or proposes an alternative. Both receive reminders and can reschedule under the configured policy. After the appointment, staff record attendance, an outcome and the next task. No-show and cancellation reasons support operations reporting without automatically publishing negative customer reviews.
Confidential visits
For a confidential business, the exact address can be disclosed only after approval. The organizer may provide discreet arrival instructions. The customer should see whether staff or customers at the business are aware of the proposed transaction.
Example
Maya requests a cafÃ© visit for 10 November at 3 pm in the listing's local time zone. The broker proposes 4 pm. Maya accepts and receives a confirmed invitation. Her dashboard displays the appointment in her own time zone and clearly names the venue time zone too.
Integration and fallback
Calendar synchronization and video meeting creation are optional provider integrations. Basic scheduling must still work inside NashBizHub if an external calendar disconnects. A failed invitation delivery should be visible to staff and retried without creating a second appointment.
Completion standard
Two customers cannot reserve the same exclusive slot simultaneously. Rescheduling updates the existing booking and its reminders rather than leaving the old meeting active.

Negotiation behavior
The recipient can accept the current version, decline, request clarification or make a counterproposal. Each new version preserves the earlier record. An acceptance must identify the exact version, actor and timestamp. An expired or withdrawn offer cannot be accepted through an old link.
The interface must distinguish agreement on preliminary commercial terms from an executed agreement and a completed transaction. Whether an offer can itself be binding depends on the approved workflow and wording for that market; the software must not assume all offers have the same legal effect.
Multiple interested parties
Owners may receive several offers. Each party sees only its own negotiation. If an exclusive stage is agreed, the system records its start, expiry and effect on other applications. Simultaneous acceptance requests must not create two incompatible awards for one exclusive opportunity.
Example
Maya offers USD 395,000 for Riverside CafÃ© with specific conditions. The owner counters at USD 405,000 and changes the training period. Maya accepts version 2. The system opens the transaction checklist while leaving the listing Under Negotiation until completion is recorded.
Completion standard
Notifications summarize the changed terms and link to the authoritative version. Email replies do not silently amend a structured offer without confirmation in the workspace.
19 Agreements due diligence and handover
After terms progress, the parties need a shared checklist of documents, decisions and outstanding conditions. Each task should have an owner, due date, evidence and completion status.
Due diligence workspace
Configure task templates by transaction type. A business sale may require financial review, asset confirmation, premises arrangements, permissions, stock reconciliation and transition planning. Rentals emphasize operating rights, condition records and responsibility allocation. Contracts emphasize scope, deliverables, reporting and acceptance criteria.
Questions can be assigned to the relevant party with supporting attachments. A response can be marked reviewed, needing clarification or accepted. Completing a checklist item records a decision; it does not claim that NashBizHub independently certified the underlying business.
Agreement preparation
Use approved templates or upload an adviser-prepared agreement. Store draft versions, reviewers, signatories and signing order. Material changes after signing require a new version or recorded amendment rather than replacing the executed file. External signature status must be confirmed through the provider or a verified manual record.
Handover
Record inventory, assets, keys, access accounts, training sessions, premises condition, meter readings, outstanding tasks and receiving-party acknowledgement. Passwords should be transferred through an approved secure method, not stored in public notes or ordinary document attachments.
Example
Riverside CafÃ© has twelve handover tasks. Eleven are complete, but premises consent remains outstanding. The workspace displays the blocking task and prevents an ordinary user from marking the transaction completed. A privileged exception requires a reason and notification under the approved policy.
Completion record
Store actual completion date, authorized confirmation, relevant evidence and public listing outcome. Send appropriate notices, pause conflicting offers and retain the private record according to policy. Failed transactions can reopen the listing after owner confirmation without deleting the unsuccessful deal history.
Boundary
The platform coordinates records and signatures. Settlement, ownership registration and adviser review remain with the responsible parties unless a separately approved service is implemented.
20 Ongoing rental and contract management
Rentals and management agreements continue after the initial handover. They need an operational workspace rather than disappearing into a completed listing archive.
Agreement register
Store agreement reference, parties, linked listing, effective date, expiry, renewal options, notice dates, payment schedule, responsibility matrix and responsible manager. Agreement status may be Pending Start, Active, Notice Given, Renewal Pending, Completed, Terminated or Disputed.
Obligations and milestones
Create recurring responsibilities such as monthly reports, inspections, insurance evidence renewal, maintenance checks and service deliverables. Each item has a due date, accountable party, supporting evidence and review decision. Missed items generate reminders and, where appropriate, escalation.
Payment records
Record expected amount, period, due date, reported payment date, receipt evidence and verification status. Manual records are labeled Reported or Confirmed; they are not treated as gateway-confirmed transactions. Partial payments, disputes and adjustments need separate entries rather than overwriting the original obligation.
Maintenance and variations
An operator can report an asset fault, attach photographs and request action. The agreed responsibility determines who receives it. Changes to duties, duration or commercial terms create an amendment request with both parties' approval as required. Historical agreement versions remain available.
Renewal and return
Reminders precede notice deadlines. The parties can renew, negotiate or plan handback. End-of-term tasks include condition inspection, asset count, access removal, unresolved issues and deposit instructions for the responsible holder. A reminder must not automatically renew or terminate an agreement.
Example
The laundromat operator reports machine 14 as unavailable. The owner reviews the maintenance obligation, assigns a repair and records completion. At month 21, both parties receive a reminder to review the 24-month arrangement before the contractual notice deadline.
Completion standard
Advertising expiry, account membership expiry and agreement expiry remain separate events with separate consequences.
21 Brokers advisers and organization teams
Broker functionality should support professional representation without allowing access to unrelated owners' information. Organizations can manage several agents, branches and assigned portfolios.
Broker profile and directory
Approved public profiles show name, company, service regions, specialties, biography, languages, applicable credentials and active public listings. Customers can search by location and industry or request assistance. Badges explain the specific check performed and do not claim guaranteed transaction success.
Owner appointment
An owner can invite a broker or request a match. The platform records acceptance, representation scope, responsible agent and any expiry. The owner can retain agreed visibility over inquiries and progress. Ending representation stops future access while retaining records and an orderly reassignment process.
Team workspace
Managers can assign listings, distribute leads, set follow-up targets, review activity and reallocate work during leave. Agents can create drafts on behalf of authorized owners. Bulk import is available through a controlled template with preview, validation, duplicate detection and row-level error results.
Adviser collaboration
An accountant, lawyer or other adviser can be invited to a specific transaction with limited access. They should not require a full broker account simply to review an assigned document or task. The inviter and the adviser can see the boundaries of the invitation.
Example
Summit Business Partners has three agents. Agent A manages Riverside CafÃ©. Agent B covers a visit while Agent A is away but cannot browse unrelated confidential offers. The manager can reassign the lead with a recorded reason.
Professional services referrals
An optional directory can introduce finance providers, accountants or other advisers. Referral consent must identify the recipient and purpose. A customer inquiry should not be distributed to multiple third parties through an unrelated newsletter checkbox.
Completion standard
Bulk imports and team changes obey the same ownership, moderation and confidentiality rules as individual records.
22 Staff CRM tasks and follow up
The internal customer relationship workspace gives NashBizHub staff a complete operational view of assigned customers and opportunities. It should distinguish customer records, individual inquiries and actual transactions.
Customer record
Maintain name, organization, verified contact channels, interests, consent preferences, acquisition source, assigned adviser, tags and relationship history. One customer may have several inquiries and deals. Duplicate detection suggests potential matches, but merges require review and preserve original references.
Lead pipeline
A proposed inquiry pipeline is New, Assigned, Contact Attempted, Responded, Qualified, Meeting Arranged, Negotiation, Converted or Closed. A closed lead includes a reason such as not suitable, no response, duplicate or customer withdrew. A qualified lead is not automatically a completed transaction.
Tasks and follow up
Staff can create calls, reminders, document requests, meeting preparation and follow-up tasks with due dates, priorities and owners. Managers see overdue work and can reassign it. Automation can create a follow-up after an unanswered inquiry or notify a manager about an overdue high-priority case.
Activity history
The record shows messages, calls recorded by staff, appointments, status changes and linked support cases in time order. Staff notes remain internal. Sensitive notes have narrower access and should not appear in general spreadsheet exports.
Example
A customer asks about two businesses. The CRM holds one customer, two inquiry records and separate next actions. Closing the first inquiry as unsuitable does not close the second or remove the customer from the database.
Operational controls
Bulk actions require preview and confirmation. Imports show source and permission to use the data. Exports are permission controlled and logged. Automated messages respect customer consent and contact preferences rather than using every CRM entry as a marketing audience.
Completion standard
Every active inquiry has a responsible person or queue, and every overdue action can be identified without opening each record individually.
23 Support complaints reviews and disputes
Support should help customers resolve account, listing and transaction-process issues while keeping the platform's role clear.
Support tickets
Customers select a topic, describe the problem, link relevant listing or agreement references and attach safe supporting evidence. Each ticket receives a reference, priority, assigned team, response target and status. Suggested states are Open, Assigned, Waiting for Customer, Waiting for Third Party, Resolved and Closed.
Reports and moderation
Visitors and members can report misleading information, duplicate listings, suspected unauthorized advertising or abusive contact. Reports enter a review queue. Staff can request evidence, temporarily restrict visibility, notify the advertiser and record an outcome. A report is an allegation until reviewed, not an automatic public finding.
Dispute records
Participants can raise a dispute about access, communication, an obligation or a recorded payment. The system preserves relevant versions and documents, assigns a reviewer and tracks responses. It should not automatically determine legal liability, release externally held deposits or alter executed agreements.
Reviews
If reviews are enabled, eligibility should be tied to a defined interaction. Users can rate communication or service using structured criteria, provide comments and allow a response. Reviews are moderated for private information and abuse. A negative review cannot expose a confidential business identity or document.
Example
An operator disputes a maintenance responsibility. The support case links the agreement version and relevant task. Both parties provide evidence. Staff record assistance and the agreed next step without silently rewriting the contract.
Escalation and appeal
Policies define which issues go to an operational manager, security reviewer or external adviser. Customers can request review of a moderation decision. Suspension notices explain the applicable reason and available appeal route where appropriate.
Completion standard
Ticket access follows the customer's relationship to the record. A support reference alone must not allow another user to retrieve the conversation or its attachments.

25 Memberships promotions and platform billing
The system can support a commercial model without fixing prices in this proposal. Management can configure products, access limits and advertising benefits after the operating model is approved.
Proposed product types
Products may include individual listing packages, broker portfolios, featured placements, buyer research membership and additional listing allowances. Each product needs a name, benefits, duration, eligibility and clear start and end rules. Paid promotion changes exposure, not verification status or moderation priority unless explicitly described.
Membership lifecycle
A customer selects a product, reviews terms and uses the approved payment provider. The system records Pending, Active, Payment Action Required, Overdue, Cancelled or Expired status as appropriate. Renewal reminders and cancellation consequences are shown clearly. Existing private transaction records must not vanish merely because promotion expires.
Billing records
The finance workspace supports invoices, provider references, receipts, adjustment records, refunds and reconciliation. A refund request, provider processing and confirmed refund are distinct states. Repeated payment notifications must not issue duplicate entitlements or receipts.
Important separation
Platform advertising and membership payments are separate from business purchase funds, rental deposits, operating rent or contractor settlement. The base proposal does not place those transaction funds into a NashBizHub wallet. If future collection or escrow is required, use an approved provider and a separately designed operational process.
Example
A broker activates a portfolio product and promotes three listings. One listing remains Under Review, so its paid entitlement is recorded but it is not published until approved. Ending the promotion removes featured placement while preserving authorized deal history.
Optional commercial controls
Management can enable trials, promotional codes, credits and sponsored category placements. Each has eligibility, expiry and audit rules. Promotions cannot bypass account restrictions or turn an unverified business into a verified one.

28 Optional expansion modules

Valuation and research

A confidentiality agreement setting out how information may be used
Due diligence
The checks a party performs before committing to or completing a transaction
Document room
A private area containing files for approved participants
Revenue
Business income from sales before deducting expenses
EBITDA
Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization; not the same as cash available to an owner
SDE or owner benefit
A stated owner-oriented earnings measure that requires an explanation of adjustments
Asset sale
A proposed transfer of specified business assets rather than an automatic transfer of the whole company
CRM
The staff system for customer relationships, inquiries and follow-up work
Moderation
Reviewing submitted content before or after publication
Verification
A specific check of an account, document or authority claim
Integration or API
A controlled connection through which two software services exchange information
Webhook
An automatic notification from another service when an event happens
Background job
Work processed separately so that the customer does not wait on the screen
Audit trail
A history showing who performed an important action and when
Two-factor authentication
A second identity check in addition to the password
Acceptance criteria
Demonstrable conditions that show a feature works as agreed
Example of the distinctions
Maya's first message is an inquiry. After the owner reviews her suitability, it may become a qualified inquiry. Her accepted preliminary terms create a progressing deal. A signed agreement and completed handover support the final transaction outcome. None of these stages should be reported as another stage merely to increase dashboard totals.
Reading financial information
Financial labels should always identify the period, currency, source and any adjustments. The platform can organize these records and calculations, but customer and adviser review is still needed to understand what those figures mean for a particular opportunity.
